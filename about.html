About Roshkunjo

A little sweetness, a lot of tradition.

Our Story

Bengal has a special relationship with sweets. From family gatherings to festivals, mishti has always been a part of our happiest memories.

Roshkunjo celebrates this tradition by bringing together the familiar flavours and timeless favourites that make Bengali sweets so special.

Our aim is simple: to make every visit a pleasant experience and every sweet moment memorable.

What Matters to Us

Quality

We believe good sweets begin with good ingredients.

Tradition

We celebrate the flavours Bengal has loved for generations.

Happiness

We believe sweets are best when shared with others.
